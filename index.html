Questions

Everything You're Wondering

Will my customers know it's AI?

Yes, and that's a good thing. It always tells callers it's a virtual assistant. What callers care about is that someone picks up right away, answers their question and gets them booked. If they'd rather talk to a person, it transfers the call to you or takes a message.

Does it sound robotic?

No. It uses natural, modern AI voices and talks like a friendly front-desk person. The best way to judge is to hear it: ask for your free demo, and call it yourself.

What if a caller asks something it doesn't know?

It's set up not to guess. It takes a message with all the details and texts it to you right away, or transfers the caller to you if you prefer.

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls to it: all calls, after hours only, or only the ones your team doesn't pick up.

Can it handle urgent calls?

Yes. You tell us what counts as urgent (a patient in pain, a burst pipe, a client who needs help today), and it transfers those calls straight to you or whoever is on call.

Does it work with my calendar?

It works with Google Calendar and Calendly, and we can connect many booking tools and CRMs, like Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan. Using something else? Ask us, and we'll tell you honestly whether we can connect it.

How long does setup take, and what do I have to do?

About 5 days. Your part is a 15-minute call and testing it when it's ready. We build, set up and fine-tune everything.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many calls you get and what you need it to do. Tell us about your business and we'll give you a simple, honest quote, with no pressure. The best first step is your free custom demo, so you can hear it before you decide.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month-to-month. If it isn't bringing you customers, you shouldn't keep paying for it.
